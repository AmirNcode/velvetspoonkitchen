About Mandana

Mandana is the cook, planner, and quiet host behind Velvet Spoon Kitchen. What began as cooking for family and close friends has grown into a personal catering service for small celebrations across Vancouver.

She keeps each event intentionally hands-on: planning the menu, preparing the food, and adding the finishing touches herself so every gathering feels cared for rather than catered at scale.
